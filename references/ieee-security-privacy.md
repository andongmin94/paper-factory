# IEEE Security & Privacy feature article

Choose this venue when the completed contribution concerns a substantive security or privacy problem that practitioners can act on. Make the relevant actors, assets, authority or trust boundaries, threat assumptions, alternatives, mitigations, and residual risks explicit. Do not turn a general integration issue into a security claim merely to fill a second venue.
