# Paper Factory

Lead the research and writing directly in the current Codex conversation. Produce the strongest useful article supported by the repository and primary research, with its actual limits visible. The scripts in this skill prepare and inspect authored files; they do not choose the research contribution or write the paper.

## Research and write

Start from the user's question, repository, retained material, and authorized scope. Inspect actual sources, dependencies, documentation, and existing analysis before selecting a consequential research or practitioner question. Read nearby primary work that could challenge the contribution and compare competent alternatives on the same supported cases. A search result, abstract, landing page, or partial extraction is not a complete primary paper. Retain source revision, exact witnesses, original retrieved bytes, URLs, dates, hashes, and any unread or inaccessible portions.

When the useful contribution is unclear, use [contribution-selection.md](references/contribution-selection.md) to compare two or three plausible questions by available evidence, the strongest existing answer and what changes for the reader. Select one before full drafting; this short choice does not add a mandatory stage or a second manuscript.

Use [research-and-review.md](references/research-and-review.md) for evidence boundaries, revision decisions, and the independent assessment rubric. Source inspection is not an executed experiment or an observed deployment outcome. Do not execute the input project's code, build, or tests unless the user authorized it. Choose further research for a concrete unanswered need that could change the article; stop when available evidence cannot support a worthwhile claim.

Choose the venue supported by the completed contribution and read its guidance:

- [IEEE Software](references/ieee-software.md): engineering decisions, alternatives, costs, and lessons.
- [IEEE Security & Privacy](references/ieee-security-privacy.md): substantive security or privacy problems with meaningful threat or authority boundaries.
- [IEEE Access](references/ieee-access.md): completed engineering research with supported methods and results.
- [JORS](references/jors.md): a Software Metapaper explaining reusable, publicly available research software, its quality control, availability, and reuse.
- [JOSS](references/joss.md): substantial research software with demonstrated use and sustained public development; produce its Markdown/BibTeX source package.

Do not treat a generic application as research software without evidence or manufacture additional articles to fill supported venues. Support does not establish a user's submission target. Access and JORS require an explicitly supplied official DOCX; JOSS uses authored YAML metadata and its own bibliography path.

Write one complete English manuscript in the selected article category unless the user requests a different language or scope. Explain its contribution, evidence, comparison, limitations, accurate references, and truthful AI-use disclosure using the venue's structure. Keep collection logs, scores, missing human information, and process notes outside the article. Never invent measurements, novelty, quotations, citations, author biography, funding, consent, or model identity.

Lead with the reader's concrete problem and the supported contribution. Demonstrate the additional value over the closest existing answer through evidence and decisions. Before freezing it, use the author editorial pass in [research-and-review.md](references/research-and-review.md) to remove defensive framing and repetition while retaining material qualifications and a useful actionable recap. Do not replace weak evidence or an unclear contribution with smoother prose.

## Revise and assess once

Apply the selected venue's current AI policy. For JOSS, limit automated assessment to author-side diagnostics; formal reviewer judgments and publication verdicts belong to human reviewers. Do not generate conversational interactions with its editors or reviewers except translation. Record actual AI tools, scope, and human verification without asserting checks or approval that did not occur.

For an authorized revision, distinguish defects repairable with retained evidence from changes needing new observations or human information. Select changes for what readers can now verify or decide. Where advice needs mechanics, work through concrete state, transitions, outcomes, and refusal conditions; label proposed designs separately from implemented or evaluated results. Pinpoint consequential source evidence and identify a supplied companion by filename or stable supplement reference. Keep a compact change-and-evidence note outside the article.

For workflows combining different verdict or proof types, trace each type's applicability and replacement rules separately, including consequential bindings it does not check. Explain the differences in the worked case rather than carrying an owner, environment or freshness guarantee from one branch into another.

Judge success by the strongest useful manuscript the project and primary evidence can support, aiming for an internal recommendation to publish the article as written. Assess the actual contribution, evidence, comparison and argument against that stronger standard; being worth submitting or needing only minor revision does not meet the as-written target. Report the publication recommendation and remaining revision severity separately from submission suitability, as defined in [research-and-review.md](references/research-and-review.md). This is not a guarantee or prediction of the journal's first decision. Do not target a numerical score or inflate claims, invent observations or pad the article to force a fit. Missing research or an inaccessible source is an unresolved evidence gap, not proof of an intrinsic project ceiling.

Obtain at most one independent full-article review per invocation, using a separate agent when available and observing the JOSS diagnostic-only restriction above. Supply the exact complete article and relevant raw source and primary bodies, not a desired score or winner. Assess whether the intended reader can understand the result and use it, as well as whether it is supported; an unreadable or materially repetitive central argument can require revision even without a factual error. Where a publication recommendation is permitted, lead with that evidence-based recommendation, whether the as-written publication target is met, and consequential remaining issues; retain the no-major-revision diagnostic and separate submission recommendation for preparation tools. Numerical ratings are optional diagnostics, never a target or acceptance threshold. Preserve the actual judgment, including unfavorable findings. Do not automatically redraft, recollect, rerate, switch models, or restart after an unfavorable assessment or interruption. Further full drafting or review needs a new user request. A transparent local correction must retain the reviewed hash so an earlier review is not represented as covering changed bytes.

## Prepare and deliver

Use [tools.md](references/tools.md) for public ORCID lookup, manuscript preflight, official-template or JOSS source export, and exact-file page checks. Use the retained assets or the explicitly supplied official template required by the selected venue. Check current official guidance and preserve the guidance actually used; retained template bytes do not prove that publisher rules are unchanged.

Use [researcher-convenience.md](references/researcher-convenience.md) for portable continuation notes, a current-artifact status summary, primary-literature reading cards, claim-to-source links, editorial checks, version comparison, reusable coauthor facts, anonymous drafts, reviewer responses, and submission-file bundles. Use the local environment diagnostic when moving PCs or diagnosing document prerequisites. These optional tools do not add research stages, model calls, or automatic revision loops. Before substantial writing for an uncertain venue, explain the intended reader, article category, supported contribution, required evidence, and any fit or current-guidance gaps. This is a qualitative fit decision, not a new score or a promise of acceptance.

Treat existing raw data, manuscripts, reviews, failures, budgets, and historical counts as read-only. Put new work in a fresh user-workspace directory, outside the installed skill. Retain a compact source manifest and receipt for actual tools and model use, outputs, review hashes, limits, and preparation gaps. Do not infer exact provider-call totals or a deployed model identity that was not established.

On continuation, use the optional impact report to revisit changed sources, affected claims and explicit new-question reading choices while reusing unchanged verified bindings. After actual author and page confirmations arrive, reconcile the existing exact-file records with the optional final-preparation tool; do not overwrite old reports or infer consent to remove pending items.

Inspect every page when a final PDF is produced. JOSS export provides submission sources without Word or Inara PDF rendering; report PDF and visual checks as unperformed until separately executed. Its source package cannot use the native-Word final-preparation report. Report content quality, format checks, visual inspection, missing human preparation, and actual submission separately. Genuine author identity, affiliation, contact, venue-required biography or photographs, funding/conflicts, rights, approval, and submission decisions remain human preparation when absent. Public ORCID data is not consent. A complete draft with accurately reported gaps is a valid delivery. Do not upload, submit, or contact others without separate user authorization.
