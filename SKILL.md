# Paper Factory

Lead the research and writing directly in the current Codex conversation. Produce the strongest useful article supported by the repository and primary research, with its actual limits visible. The scripts in this skill prepare and inspect authored files; they do not choose the research contribution or write the paper.

## Research and write

Start from the user's question, repository, retained material, and authorized scope. Inspect actual sources, dependencies, documentation, and existing analysis before selecting a consequential practitioner decision. Read nearby primary work that could challenge the contribution and compare competent alternatives on the same supported cases. A search result, abstract, landing page, or partial extraction is not a complete primary paper. Retain source revision, exact witnesses, original retrieved bytes, URLs, dates, hashes, and any unread or inaccessible portions.

Use [research-and-review.md](references/research-and-review.md) for evidence boundaries, revision decisions, and the independent assessment rubric. Source inspection is not an executed experiment or an observed deployment outcome. Do not execute the input project's code, build, or tests unless the user authorized it. Choose further research for a concrete unanswered need that could change the article; stop when available evidence cannot support a worthwhile claim.

Choose the venue supported by the completed contribution: [IEEE Software](references/ieee-software.md) for engineering decisions, alternatives, costs, and lessons; [IEEE Security & Privacy](references/ieee-security-privacy.md) for a substantive security or privacy problem with meaningful threat or authority boundaries. Read only the relevant venue guidance. Do not manufacture a second article to fill both venues.

Write one complete English feature article unless the user requests a different language or scope. Its argument should carry the question, completed analysis and results, fair comparison, worked decisions, limitations, accurate references, and truthful AI-use disclosure. Keep collection logs, scores, missing human information, and process notes outside the article. Never invent measurements, novelty, quotations, citations, author biography, funding, consent, or model identity.

## Revise and assess once

For an authorized revision, distinguish defects repairable with retained evidence from changes needing new observations or human information. Select changes for what readers can now verify or decide. Where advice needs mechanics, work through concrete state, transitions, outcomes, and refusal conditions; label proposed designs separately from implemented or evaluated results. Pinpoint consequential source evidence and identify a supplied companion by filename or stable supplement reference. Keep a compact change-and-evidence note outside the article.

Aim for nine out of ten in every declared rubric criterion, without treating that aspiration as a gate or a promised result. Obtain at most one independent full-article review per invocation, using a separate agent when available. Supply the exact complete article and relevant raw source and primary bodies, not a desired score or winner. Preserve the actual judgment, including low scores. Do not automatically redraft, recollect, rerate, switch models, or restart after a low score or interruption. Further full drafting or review needs a new user request. A transparent local correction must retain the reviewed hash so an earlier review is not represented as covering changed bytes.

## Prepare and deliver

Use [tools.md](references/tools.md) for public ORCID lookup, manuscript preflight, official-template export, and exact-file page checks. The skill directory contains its own scripts and official assets. Check the selected publisher's current official guidance and preserve the guidance actually used; retained template bytes do not prove that publisher rules are unchanged.

Use [researcher-convenience.md](references/researcher-convenience.md) for portable continuation notes, a current-artifact status summary, primary-literature reading cards, claim-to-source links, editorial checks, version comparison, reusable coauthor facts, anonymous drafts, reviewer responses, and submission-file bundles. Use the local environment diagnostic when moving PCs or diagnosing document prerequisites. These optional tools do not add research stages, model calls, or automatic revision loops. Before substantial writing for an uncertain venue, explain the intended reader, article category, supported contribution, required evidence, and any fit or current-guidance gaps. This is a qualitative fit decision, not a new score or a promise of acceptance.

Treat existing raw data, manuscripts, reviews, failures, budgets, and historical counts as read-only. Put new work in a fresh user-workspace directory, outside the installed skill. Retain a compact source manifest and receipt for actual tools and model use, outputs, review hashes, limits, and preparation gaps. Do not infer exact provider-call totals or a deployed model identity that was not established.

Inspect every page of the exact final PDF. Report content quality, format checks, visual inspection, missing human preparation, and actual submission separately. Genuine author identity, affiliation, contact, biography, photographs, funding/conflicts, rights, approval, and submission decisions remain human preparation when absent. Public ORCID data is not consent. A complete draft with accurately reported gaps is a valid delivery. Do not upload, submit, or contact others without separate user authorization.
